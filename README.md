## 📌 Project Overview
**Neighborhood Watch Companion** is a community-driven mobile application designed to enhance local safety and communication.  
The app allows users to:
- Report incidents in real time  
- View incidents reported by others in their area on an interactive map  
- Receive timely notifications about events that matter to them  

By providing a centralized platform for sharing information, the app aims to foster a stronger, more vigilant community.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🏡 Home Screen
The **Home Screen** provides a personalized dashboard at a glance.  
Features include:
- **Neighborhood-Based View:** Automatically identifies and displays the user’s neighborhood.  
- **User Greeting:** A personalized welcome message.  
- **Incident Statistics:** Shows weekly incidents and total incidents in the neighborhood.  
- **Recent Incidents List:** Chronological list of the most recent incidents with type, date, time, and description.

---

### 📝 Report Incident
The **Report Incident** feature allows users to quickly share important information.  

Steps:  
1. **Select Incident Type:** Choose from predefined categories (e.g., Accident, Theft, Vandalism).  
2. **Add Details:** Title, description, date, and time.  
3. **Specify Location:** Auto-detect location or adjust manually on the map.  
4. **Upload Media:** Add one or more images.  
5. **Submit:** Review and share with the community.  

---

### 🗺️ Map View
The **Map View** visualizes incidents in the user’s area.  
- **Interactive Map:** Centered on the user’s neighborhood.  
- **Incident Markers:** Clear icons showing location and type of incidents.  
- **Quick Information:** Tap markers for a summary, with an option to view full details.  

---

### 🔍 Incident Details
Provides a full breakdown of each report.  
Includes:  
- **Location Map:** Pinpoints the exact location.  
- **Incident Information:** Title, description, date/time.  
- **Attached Images:** Gallery of uploaded images.  

---

### ⚙️ Settings
Gives users control over their app experience.  
- **Account Management** – Manage personal account details.  
- **Notifications** – Enable/disable push and email alerts.  
- **Privacy Control** – Toggle location sharing.  
- **About & Support** – Contact info and app details.  
- **Logout** – Securely sign out.  

---

## 🔄 User Flow
1. **Welcome & Login:** User logs in.  
2. **Home Screen:** Displays neighborhood overview.  
3. **Reporting:** Tap “Report Incident” to share information.  
4. **Viewing Incidents:** Browse incidents from the list or via the map.  
5. **Settings:** Customize preferences, privacy, and notifications.
